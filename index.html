Growing blocks

mL/h
Measure the CO₂ per block on your farm
  1. Stop the exhaust fan and close the room.
  2. Record the CO₂ rise in ppm per minute over 10 min.
  3. Calculate: CO₂ per block (mL/h) = rise (ppm/min) × room volume (m³) × 60 ÷ number of blocks.

Example: 25 ppm/min × 60 m³ × 60 ÷ 2,000 blocks = 45 mL/h. The value changes with the stage, so measure it again at each stage.

Sensors

The controller decides only when a new reading comes in. Between readings, the machines keep the last command.

A spare set must be next to the main set. In another position it measures other air, and a take-over gives a wrong value.

Check an RH sensor (salt test)
  1. Put the sensor in a closed box with a cup of wet table salt (sodium chloride). Keep the temperature stable.
  2. After 6–12 h, the sensor must read 75 %. Repeat with potassium sulfate: 97 %.
  3. Enter the difference as the sensor correction in "Machine protection and safe states".

Above 95 % RH, normal sensors can stay wet and read 100 %. A heated probe prevents this. Protect the CO₂ sensor from condensation too.

For machines in Auto. Priority 1 acts before priority 2, and so on.

Above the humidity rule. A critical alarm comes on.

Priority 2 · Humidity first
Priority 3 · CO₂ purge

Steady: the fan runs at Low in pinning and fruiting and goes one level up or down each 5 min with CO₂. No bursts of fresh air.

Priority 3 · Air conditioner during a purge

Test result: with no AC rule, the compressor starts about once a day. Measure one restart before you use this rule.

Fixed values (not settings)

Steady fresh air: base level Low in pinning and fruiting, off in spawn run and rest, one step each 5 min. Humidity first acts only after the fogger is at its maximum for 5 min. Alarms need their condition for 5 min (critical alarms 60 s). Fan: minimum 2 min on and 2 min off. Fogger: 1 min. AC: 2 min between commands. The fogger never stops for a purge and goes one level higher. Fog cools first when the room is warm. The AC fan speed follows humidity. The circulation fan runs with the fog and 10 min of each 30 min. A setback ends after 30 min or at a temperature alarm. In spawn run, the AC also cools for the block core. Humidity control uses VPD.
