# Tasks as a grid: UI/UX critique and audit (2026-11-05)

Method: Impeccable critique and audit, run in one context: a teammate working from a brief, with the lead's single-table format. Assessment A was the design review of the live pages. Assessment B was the detector: a file scan of `web/task-grid*.js`, `web/task-sheet.js` and `web/tasks-card.js` (0 findings; it reads JS by regex only), plus URL scans of `#/rooms/2/tasks` and the month sheet at 1280 and 390 px. B ran after A, so its results did not shape the review.

Test bench: this worktree's server on port 18743 with a throwaway database. Seed:

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Container 1 | Grey oyster, fruiting |
| Container 2 | Grey oyster plus lion's mane, fruiting, with an own daily rule |
| Container 3 | No crops |
| Workers | Ali Lee, Sara, and Amin Lau (Ali Lee and Amin Lau clash as "AL") |
| Past rows | 28 Sep – 4 Nov: done (kg and bags amounts), missed, skipped with reasons, cancelled |
| Today | Partly done |

Screens were checked at 1440, 1024, 700, 390 and 360 px, in both themes. The month sheet was printed to PDF through CDP (`Page.printToPDF`, the page's own `@page`). Screenshots and PDFs are in `/tmp/tg-audit/shots/`. A long press under real touch was not exercised: right-click and `contextmenu` were dispatched with an emulated touch viewport. Tap Done, the Done dialog with the pick check, Undo and Details were all run for real.

## Verdict

The grid does what the owner asked. It reads like the paper log: days down, checks across, ticks and initials. On a desk a worker sees today's gaps at once: the empty open boxes have a 2 px outline inside the accent ring. The owner can find missed days by the amber ✗, the grid's only colour. The phone turn (tasks down, days across) is the right call and fits 360 px without sideways scroll. The box, menu and dialog code paths are shared. The house style holds: Inter, tabular figures, hairlines, one filled button, the task pictures.

It is not yet polished, for four reasons:

1. **The top bar breaks from 601 to about 900 px.** At 700 px the page scrolls sideways by 33 px, and the brand mark is squeezed to 22 px under "Live". This affects every page, not only Tasks.
2. **Numbers have no units.** "✓ 17.22" and "✓ 2" (kg or bags?) appear on the grid and on the sheet.
3. **The month sheet is not yet a sheet you can write on.** In landscape the rows are 4.3 mm tall with pill-shaped boxes, and a quarter of the page is empty. "✓ 19.59 Amin" runs into the next column. On paper a missed day is the faintest mark on the page.
4. **Narrow desks (601 to about 800 px) break column names mid-word** ("Contaminat / ion walk").

There is also one interaction gap: a tap is Done at once, but nothing on screen says it can be undone.
