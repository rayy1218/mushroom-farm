# Inventory: UI/UX critique and audit (2026-11-05)

Method: Impeccable critique and audit, degraded to one context (a single auditor working from the lead's brief, in the lead's one-table format). Assessment A was the design review of the live pages and the code. Assessment B was the detector, run after A, so it did not shape the review. The file scan of the seven `web/inventory-*.js` files found 0 (it reads JS by regex only). The detector's own URL scans timed out after 30 s on every address, because the server-sent-events stream never lets the network go idle. So `detect.js` was injected into the audit tab (live server, `--keep-inject`, no project file touched) on the list, item, count, sheet and Settings pages, at 1440 and 390 px.

Test bench: this worktree (`feat/inventory`, be9e184) on port 18960, with a throwaway database at `/tmp/iv-audit/audit.db` and the gateway pointed at a dead port. Seed:

Every dialog was opened, and these were run for real: Record use, Record anyway, Throw out, Edit a throw-out, Delete, Archive, Add item with a taken name, and Count stock with a bad figure and a save. Touch was emulated (a mobile viewport with touch), not tested on a phone.

## Verdict

Inventory looks like the rest of the app. It reuses the Harvest page's head, cards, `us-table`, `us-tiles`, the task dialogs (unit inside the box, the day in words under the date, the worker picker), the Harvest pick check ("Only 1.20 L in stock. Record 5.00 L anyway?" in amber), and the 4-6 sheet's print page. The State-Only Colour Rule holds: amber for Low and use-by, red for Expired, a grey Archived pill, and "not counted" in muted text, never amber. All figures use tabular numbers and one formatter, and they stay legible at 99,999.74 L and 11,980 pcs. Nothing scrolls sideways at 360 px except the sheet (#18). Touch targets are 44 px on a phone. Contrast passes in both themes: muted text 5.6 : 1 dark and 5.0 : 1 light; amber pill text 10.3 and 5.2.

The weak spot is the floor worker's two fast jobs:

1. **Record use opens on the wrong item.** The picker opens on the first item of the list, a block item. A worker who types a figure without checking the picker records it against that item. On the bench, 5 blocks came off "Grey oyster blocks" this way. The over-stock check stayed quiet, because 5 is under 580.
2. **Nothing says a record was saved.** After Record use, Purchase, Throw out or Save counts, the dialog or page closes and a figure somewhere changes. On a phone that figure is usually off screen. There is no Undo.
3. **Count stock loses a half-done count.** The back link, a top-bar tap or the phone's Back discards every typed box without a word, and Save is at the foot of a 1,730 px page.

On the owner's side, the In stock tile and the Expired pill paint a whole item red when only one lot has expired (#4). Smaller finds: the desk list spreads the item's name and its numbers 600 to 900 px apart, the history's figures do not line up, and the printed sheet's write-in columns are too narrow to write in.
