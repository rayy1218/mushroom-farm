## Verdict

The page is authored for this farm, not a template. It is one room and crop, against the four ways that matter for that crop. The ways left out say why ("Cameron Highlands averages 17.9 °C"). Every figure says where it came from and links its sources. The headline gives the answer in one sentence, in the app's money format. Work it out runs the farm's own simulator and says plainly that nothing is switched. The states hold up: the restart failure, Not saved with Retry, the gone room and the refusals all say what happened in plain words, in the right tone, and keep the cursor in its row.

The weak spot is the page's core promise: "Change a figure and the page works it out again as you type." The owner types in the Figures card, but the answer changes 1,000 to 3,000 px above, so it is never seen while typing (#1). Next, the owner loses the context for a figure once it is set: the research range goes (#5), nothing questions an unlikely figure (#6), and a refused box leaves an unsaved figure in the headline (#7).
